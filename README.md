# Bike Buyers Analysis — Excel Dashboard Project
# Project Overview
This project analyses a dataset of 1,000 customers to uncover the demographic and behavioural patterns that predict whether a customer will purchase a bike. Using Microsoft Excel, the raw data was cleaned, transformed, analysed using Pivot Tables, and visualised in an interactive Dashboard — demonstrating the full data analysis workflow from raw data to business insight.
# Goal
Identify who is most likely to buy a bike and why, so a business can target the right customers with the right message.
# Key Insights from the Analysis
Income matters - Customers who purchased bikes had noticeably higher average incomes across both genders.

Commute distance is a driver - Short to medium commuters (0–5 miles) are the most likely buyers — bikes serve a practical transport need.

Middle age dominates purchases - Customers aged 31–54 account for the largest proportion of bike purchases.

Single customers buy more - Single customers showed a higher purchase rate than married customers, possibly due to lifestyle flexibility.

Region differences exist - Purchase patterns varied across Europe, Pacific, and North America regions.

Car ownership reduces purchase likelihood - Customers with more cars were less likely to purchase a bike, suggesting cars serve as substitutes.
